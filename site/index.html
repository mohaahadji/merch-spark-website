A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Curious?
You’re in good company.

Is the extension the same as the research website?

They work together. The extension supports sales and catalog workflows inside Merch on Demand. The research website contains Product Discovery and Keyword Hunter, and connects to the extension for Amazon product collection.

Do I need the extension to collect research?

Yes. Open Research Studio in Chrome with the updated Spark extension and enable Amazon.com research in its settings. The studio also supports browser-local file imports. Contact the creator about extension access.

Are purchase badges exact sales numbers?

No. A badge such as “100+ bought in past month” is Amazon’s reported lower bound. BSR is a category-relative snapshot. Neither guarantees future sales, royalties, or profitability.

Where does my research go?

Your captured research and shortlist stay in your browser. The research website does not receive your private Merch account reports or Ads credentials. Read the privacy and data details.
