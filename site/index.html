Bring Spark to your research.

This workspace connects to the Merch Spark extension in your browser. Amazon does not allow this website to read its pages directly.

  1. Load the updated Merch Spark extension v3.2.0 or newer in Chrome.
  2. Open its settings and click Enable Amazon.com research.
  3. Refresh this website, then click Connect Spark.
Your private extension source ↗

No Amazon Ads API key is needed for public product research. If a search is blocked, open it on Amazon, complete any browser verification yourself, and use Capture open tab.

Your Amazon research source

Your supplied novelty-shirt search is the starting point. Seller and keyword filters are retained when searching niches.

Bring your research with you.

Import CSV or JSON with asin, title, price, rating, reviewCount, bsr, bsrCategory, image. Only ASIN and title are required. Rows marked sponsored are excluded; unknown fields remain blank.

Imports are browser-local. Data is not uploaded to a server. Imported rows reflect the file you provide, not a fresh Amazon lookup.

A model, not a sales report.

Amazon’s BSR is relative to a category. It does not publish competitors’ unit sales. Enable your own model only when you have a suitable reference for US Clothing, Shoes & Jewelry.

units = reference units × (reference BSR ÷ product BSR)exponent

This is a user-calibrated heuristic, not a validated Amazon sales estimator. The displayed 0.5×–2× band is a scenario range, not a statistical confidence interval. Estimated royalties use your input, not the product owner’s earnings. Non-Clothing ranks are excluded.
